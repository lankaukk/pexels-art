How the sexes were guessed: red color is carried on the sex chromosome, and a mother passes hers only to sons. With a red mom and a gray dad, red babies are male and gray babies are female. Wilburt looks mostly white, but white is a separate gene that just switches color off in patches. Wherever his color does show through, like the peachy wash on his head and wings, it's warm, so his base color counts as red. It also means Breanna's father had to be red.
